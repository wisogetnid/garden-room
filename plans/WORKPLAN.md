# SITE MANAGER'S WORKPLAN: Cowes High-Performance EPS-Core SIPS Workshop (3.6 x 5.116m)

**Status:** ACTIVE BUILD SCHEDULE  
**Foreman:** The DIY Project Coach  
**Target:** 14.53m² Net Usable Footprint (16.78m² Structural Core / 18.42m² Gross External) via 125mm Wall / 125mm Roof EPS-Core SIPs  
**Boundary Basis:** Relocated to ≥1000mm from the North, South and East boundaries measured to the outermost projection (roof oversail + fascia + gutter). Core order size **3408 x 4924mm**. Building sits **354mm further west** than originally set out.  

---

## 🏗️ The Foreman's Rules of Engagement

Listen up. You’re a weekend warrior with a 9-5 job, and you only have **45 to 90 minutes of daylight/energy on weeknights**. Burnout, not budget, is the biggest threat to this build. If you blow out your back on a Tuesday evening, the project stalls for a month. We operate on these strict protocols:

1.  **The 'Big Event' Protocol:** For major milestones (Ground Screws, SIP Erection Handover, Roof Insulation & EPDM), you need a 48-hour countdown checklist. These are strictly reserved for weekends. You don't do these alone. Get mates. Buy pizza.
2.  **Evening Micro-Shifts:** Weeknights are for 45-minute "setup" or "finesse" tasks. Cowes has neighbors. Tuesday nights are for 'Quiet/Low-Impact' tasks: taping the VCL, measuring, cutting insulation, or tool maintenance. No chop saws after 7 PM. 
3.  **The "Stop-Loss" Rule:** If an evening task takes more than 90 minutes or you start making tired mistakes, put the tools down. Fatigue leads to injuries and ruined materials.
4.  **The Sunday Lockdown:** At 4:00 PM every Sunday, tools go down. You execute a 'Weather-Tight & Secure' checklist to ensure the build stays dry and safe while you are at your desk from Monday to Friday. *With OSB SIPS, this means zero-delay on wrapping weather protection.*
5.  **Statutory Position:** The building is **exempt from the Building Regulations** (Schedule 2, Class VI — confirmed in writing by Building Control) and has a **Lawful Development Certificate**. There are no Building Control inspections. Keep every wall face ≥1000mm from the boundaries when setting out, and build to the LDC drawings.

## Step-by-Step Construction Phases (Overview)
1. **Phase 1: Ground Screws & Foundation Platform** (Weeks 1-3): Ground screws at -75mm U-brackets, 225mm heave void over -300mm pit, 141x125mm ring beam (-75mm to +50mm), 47x125mm C24 joists infilled with PIR, 22mm Egger Protect P5 T&G sub-deck.
2. **Phase 2: Superstructure & Thermal Jacket** (Weeks 4-6 + weather hold): **New Forest SIPs supply and erect the shell** (DPC, sole plate, anchoring, 125mm SIP walls/roof) and wrap it in a temporary breather membrane. Weather hold of variable length. Then strip in stages: 80mm flat over-roof PIR + EPDM membrane, +50mm aluminium starter track, 180mm XPS skirt (+50mm to +230mm), 60mm Wood Fibre (+230mm upwards), and Tyvek wrap.
3. **Phase 3: Glazing & Cladding** (Weeks 7-8): Quick-Connect AC wall penetration, Acoustic Double-Glazed (D-AC) uPVC windows (West @ 2078mm FFL, Clerestories @ 1995mm FFL) and ~1000x2078mm single-leaf (900mm leaf) door with 42mm timber riser block (+92mm FFL), 25mm rainscreen battens, Hardie VL cladding, rainwater system.
4. **Phase 4: Internal Thermal Floor & Passivhaus Detailing** (Weeks 9-11): Inside dry room, peel Egger Protect foil, glue 18mm OSB3 T&G directly to 22mm Egger Protect with D4 adhesive (forming rock-solid 40mm laminated timber slab), utility cable pull & airtight sealing, internal Tescon Vana VCL taping, 15mm direct-mount Fermacell, and 2mm industrial polyurethane/epoxy floor paint (FFL = +92mm).
5. **Phase 5: MEP, Climate & Final Commissioning** (Week 12+): MEP, dMVHR (Zehnder ComfoAir 70 on 60mm internal build-out, 270mm core, center at +1616.7mm Datum Zero / 1524.7mm FFL), ASHP, TT Island, machine pads, commissioning.

## 🗓️ Phase 1: Ground Screws & Timber Frame (Weeks 1-3)
*The heavy digging has been engineered out. Let the pros drive the screws.*

### Week 1: Site Prep & Ground Screws
*   **The Big Win:** You have 3 existing concrete slabs buried 300mm down. Do not dig them out. A specialist ground screw contractor will arrive and drive **14 No. 2050mm ground screws** on a base grid of **3268mm E-W × 4784mm N-S** (setting-out diagonal **~5793.5mm**), breaking out the slabs locally where a screw position falls on one, and working safely around the oak tree roots. ✅ **Final layout approved by the structural engineer (06 Oct 2026, max 29kN factored per screw):** 10 under the ring beam and 4 under the internal E-W beams. Set out every screw from the coordinate table in MASTER_PLAN §1.2.1, measured from P01 at the South-East corner, and check both diagonals before the rig starts.
*   **Weekend 1:** Clear surface vegetation and ensure the site is free of loose debris.

### Week 2: Retaining, Weed Control & Timber Joists
*   **The Pit Perimeter (Erosion Control):** Before joists make the area impassable, line the vertical dirt walls of your 300mm excavation pit (`3808x5324mm` footprint for 200mm over-dig). *The pit is already dug to the old setting-out — surplus at the North and South ends is harmless, but it must be extended **354mm westward** to suit the shifted position* with treated H4 gravel boards, placing them **flush and tight directly against the earth face**. Drive 50x50mm timber stakes into soft ground (or use galvanised L-brackets / concrete blocks over the old slabs) to hold back the earth. If your pick-mattock created uneven voids behind the boards, shovel pea gravel into the gap to backfill it tight. *Foreman Note: A **200mm working clearance** (or up to 400mm) outside your timber ring beam saves ~3.5 tonnes of digging while leaving plenty of room for drill/driver access and high-volume sub-floor cross-ventilation.*
*   **Weed & Moisture Control:** Roll out heavy-duty geotextile membrane across the entire dirt floor, folding it up the sides of your new retaining boards.
*   **Membrane Fixing (Zero-Build-Up):** Mechanically fix the membrane flat to the pit floor. Use Tapcons and penny washers to secure it into the exposed concrete slabs, and 150mm U-pegs driven into the bare earth. Apply butyl tape on transitions and seal any X-slits cut around the ground screws. This locks the membrane down without using any gravel ballast, reclaiming 28mm of vertical height.
*   **Weed Control Extension:** Extend the geotextile membrane **150mm past the external dimensions of the building** (beyond the ring beam/cladding line) and pin or glue it flat to the ground. This ensures the ventilated sub-skirt perimeter remains completely weed-free.
*   **Weekend 2:** Build the heavy 141x125mm ring beam and the two internal E-W beams (clamp 3 lengths of 47x125mm C24 together with sash clamps and laminate them with **SPAX 8 × 140mm A2 countersunk T40 screws** (preferred; or the engineer-approved **Swiftfix 8 × 140mm A4** fallback if SPAX can't deliver in time — trial two Swiftfix in an offcut and pre-drill if the outer ply splits) from alternating faces at 150mm centres (300mm per face) — 45mm from the top on the outer face, 45mm from the bottom on the inner face, ≥100mm from cut ends, 2 extra screws each side of butt joints, butt joints only over screw positions; see MASTER_PLAN §1.2 Lamination Screws. Ring beam sits from -75mm to +50mm datum) and M10 bolt it to the ground screw U-brackets at -75mm. Hang internal 47x125mm joists via Face-Fix galvanized joist hangers (fixed exclusively to the inside vertical face of the ring beam) @ 400mm centers flush with the top of the ring beam (+50mm datum). Double up the joists at heavy loads and intermediate ground screw locations. Add 30x5mm galvanised restraint straps at 600mm max centres to secure the joists and ring beam. The underside of the joists at -75mm establishes the mandatory 225mm heave void above the -300mm pit floor. *Foreman Note: Face-Fix hangers keep the top surface of the ring beam 100% flush timber, preventing metal tabs from puncturing the Polymeric DPC or pinching the SIP sole plate.*
*   **Rodent Mesh (The 'L-Shape' Defense):** Roll out the 6mmx6mm welded steel mesh. Hold the top edge flat against the outer face of the timber ring beam at +50mm (it will be clamped by the EWI starter track later). Drop the mesh down into the pit, bend it outwards 150mm-200mm into an "L-shape", and pin it flat directly to the ground (as there is no gravel ballast to bury it).
*   **Insulation Support & Drop:** Push stainless steel insulation support clips into the bottom sides of the C24 joists @ 600mm centers (or tack 25x25mm treated stop battens flush along the bottom edges) to create a mechanical stop ledge. Cut the 125mm PIR boards tightly and drop them between joists until they rest on the clips/battens. Inject low-expansion structural PU adhesive foam around all 4 perimeter edges of every panel to chemically bond the PIR to the timber joists and eliminate thermal bypassing.

### Week 3: The Weatherproof Sub-Deck
*   **Weekend 3:** Install 22mm Egger Protect P5 T&G chipboard (weatherproof peel-off foil, solo-lift ~24kg) screwed to joists at +50mm datum. This provides a rigid and weatherproof sub-deck that you can safely walk on and work from during the upcoming superstructure erection. Screw it down tightly to the joists at 300mm centers.

## 🗓️ Phase 2: Superstructure & Thermal Jacket (Weeks 4-6)
*New Forest SIPs build the shell. Your job is to hand them a perfect platform, check their work, and then never leave bare OSB exposed once you start stripping their wrap.*

### Week 4 Prep: Platform Handover to New Forest SIPs
*   **Weeknights (Quiet, 45m-1.5h):** Check the platform before NFS arrive — this is the last time a mistake is cheap. Ring beam outer footprint **3408 × 4924mm**, both diagonals equal, top of ring beam level at **+50mm** all round (±3mm). Egger Protect deck screwed down and swept. Clear a delivery and crane/handling route (Yarmouth ferry, shuttle vehicle for the final mile). Send NFS the dimensions they must hit: **outer face of the SIP walls and sole plates flush with the outer face of the ring beam** (the ~16mm ledge sits inside), East/West top plates at **+2216.7 / +2273.5mm**, 120mm roof oversail all round, and the factory-cut **270mm dMVHR core** position with its spline clearance (MASTER_PLAN §4.2.1).

### Week 4: 🚨 BIG EVENT: NFS Shell Erection & Handover
*   **NFS on site:** New Forest SIPs fit the DPC, sole plates and anchoring, erect the wall panels and roof panels with all structural connections and fixings, and wrap the shell in their **temporary breather membrane**.
*   **Your Handover Checklist (before they leave):**
    *   Walls plumb; outer faces flush with the ring beam; diagonals of the wall plan still equal.
    *   Top plate datums: East **+2216.7mm**, West **+2273.5mm**; roof falls **1:60** towards the East; roof oversail **120mm** on all four sides.
    *   Every opening (door, windows, clerestories, 270mm dMVHR core) in the right place and size — measure against MASTER_PLAN §2.7 and §4.2.1.
    *   Membrane wrap: laps shingled (upper over lower), taped closed over **every opening**, no loose flaps, and the roof covering able to shed water at 1:60.
    *   Get from NFS: the membrane **product name and rated UV-exposure period**, and their fixing schedule for the record.

### Weather Hold (Variable Length — Work Commitments)
*   The NFS wrap buys you time, not unlimited time. 🚨 *Foreman Note: a breather membrane is not a roof. On a 1:60 roof, water sits on it. Prioritise the roof PIR + EPDM as the first job after the hold, and over-sheet the roof with a tarp if the hold runs into wet or windy weeks.*
*   **Weekly walk-round (15 min, any day):** check for lifted laps, wind damage and ponding on the roof; re-tape tears with Tescon Extora. After any named storm, check the same day.
*   **UV deadline:** count the days from handover against the membrane's rated UV exposure. If you are going to overrun, sheet the walls and roof with heavy-duty tarpaulins before the deadline.

### Week 5: 🚨 BIG EVENT: Roof Insulation & Weather-Tight Roof (one dry weekend)
*   **Check the forecast:** do this only on a dry 2-day window — the roof OSB is exposed from the moment the wrap comes off.
*   **Saturday (Strip & Kerb):** Strip the NFS membrane from the **roof only**, working in sections. Confirm the SIP roof surface falls at **1:60** across the short width. Tape the external roof panel joints. Install the 80mm timber kerb batten on edge around the perimeter. If rain threatens, re-cover the bare sections with a tarp before you stop.
*   **Sunday (Weather-Tight):** Bond flat 80mm high-compressive strength PIR (Kingspan TR27) directly to the sloped top OSB skin of the roof SIP. Directly glue the 1.5mm EPDM membrane onto the PIR.
*   **Sunday Lockdown:** Install UPVC drip edge trims and kerb trims. The roof is now permanently "Weather-Tight". Leave the NFS wrap on the walls until Week 6.

### Week 6: External Insulation, Starter Track & Tyvek Wrap
*   **Stripping Rule (One Face at a Time):** Strip the NFS membrane from **one elevation only**, then insulate it and Tyvek it before you stop. Never end a day with bare OSB on a wall. Start with the windward West face while the weather is good; leave the sheltered faces for later weekends if the job splits.
*   **Starter Track & XPS Skirt:** Screw a **60mm wide Aluminium EWI Starter Track** horizontally into the SIP sole plate / ring beam, perfectly level at the **+50mm mark** (flush with the bottom of SIP wall, sandwiching the top edge of the anti-rodent mesh behind it). Mechanically fix the **60mm thick x 180mm high XPS Plinth Skirt** into the starter track (covering from +50mm to +230mm).
*   **Wood Fibre Insulation:** Mechanically fix **60mm Rigid Wood Fibre external insulation** directly to the exterior OSB of the SIP walls with the manufacturer's **temporary insulation fixings** (plastic disc + screw, 2–4 per board) — the battens that clamp it permanently don't go on until Week 8 — starting at the **+230mm mark** (resting on top of the XPS skirt). Apply Compriband expanding foam tape or a bead of vapour-permeable MS Polymer sealant along the XPS/Wood Fibre horizontal joint.
*   **dMVHR Opening in the Wood Fibre:** Before the Tyvek goes on, push a long drill bit out through the centre of the factory-cut 270mm SIP core to mark the pipe axis on the outside. Cut a matching **270mm circle** through the 60mm Wood Fibre (handsaw, insulation knife or multi-tool — it cuts easily). Cover the hole with the Tyvek for now; it is opened in Week 7.
*   **Breather Membrane:** Drape the **Tyvek Breather Membrane** completely over the Wood Fibre and XPS skirt, taping it to the front face of the 60mm aluminium starter track using Tescon Vana (*do NOT tuck into track*). That elevation is now permanently protected from coastal rain. Repeat face by face until the NFS wrap is gone.

## 🗓️ Phase 3: Glazing, Cladding & Rainwater (Weeks 7-8)

### Week 7: AC Penetration, Glazing & Threshold Detailing
*   **The AC Core Hole (Before Cladding):** Mount the internal bracket for the DIY Quick-Connect AC unit on the SIP OSB. Drill a ~65mm core hole through the SIP wall and Wood Fibre with a 2-3° downward angle to the outside for condensate drainage. Run the pipe bundle through to the outside and leave coiled. Fill the core gap with airtight foam, and tape the inside with Tescon Vana / Roflex grommet.
*   **The Service Cable Core Hole (Before Cladding):** Core a ~32mm hole through the SIP wall and Wood Fibre behind the Consumer Unit position, clear of the 1200mm spline joints, with a 1-2° fall to the outside. Seal the outside face onto the Tyvek with a Pro Clima Kaflex grommet (or Tescon Extora tape) so the hole is weather-tight before the cladding goes on. The SWA and Cat6a are run along the fence by the electrician and dressed in with a drip loop below the entry.
*   **The dMVHR Wall Pipe (Before Cladding):** Cut the Zehnder Ø250mm wall mounting pipe to the **296mm** finished wall thickness at the unit (236mm wall + 60mm internal build-out — follow the Zehnder template for exact projection). Cut a cross in the Tyvek over the hole and fold the flaps back. Slide the pipe in at a **1°–2° downward fall to the outside**, shim it level side-to-side in the 20mm annulus, and fill the SIP core and Wood Fibre gap with Illbruck FM330 non-swelling foam. Seal the Tyvek to the pipe with an **EPDM / Tescon Extora collar** so the hole is weather-tight before cladding. Cap both pipe ends against dust and rain. *Foreman Note: check the fall with a digital level before the foam cures — it cannot be corrected afterwards.*
*   **Glazing & Door Installation:** Install the six D-AC Acoustic Double Glazed uPVC fixed windows and the ~1000x2078mm single-leaf (900mm leaf), half-glazed Thermally Broken Aluminium door (standard double-glazed upper panel, insulated lower panel) into factory-cut SIP openings. Before signing off the door, check the seals (MASTER_PLAN §2.7): gaskets continuous and compressed on all four sides, and the threshold seal closing fully onto the flush +92mm threshold (no daylight visible with the door shut). **Door frame movement detail (MASTER_PLAN §2.7):** ~10mm jamb and ~15mm head gaps, jamb fixings only (packer behind each, none rigid through the head), expanding foam tape plus internal Contega with a movement fold at the head; no gun-foam-only seals. Record the opening diagonals and sill level in the site journal so they can be re-checked after the first winter and summer.
    *   *Note the handling protocol:* D-AC acoustic double glazed units (uPVC frames) are still heavy (~37 kg/m² glass dead load; the 1000x1000mm West units are ~30 kg of glass alone). 2-person handling with heavy-duty vacuum suction cups is mandatory.
    *   *Note the thermal expansion gap:* Leave a 10-12mm perimeter expansion clearance around every frame (uPVC expands ~3× more than aluminium, so a 1500mm clerestory moves ~4mm over a 40°C swing; fix the uPVC frames to the profile maker's expansion guidance), sealed with pre-compressed expanding foam tape (Compriband / Illbruck illmod 600 / TP652) and low-expansion flexible PU foam, backed by Pro Clima Tescon Vana airtight taping internally.
    *   *Preserve exact datums:* North/South/East clerestories top datum at **1995mm FFL** (verifying 129.7mm head zone under +2216.7mm plate); West panorama windows (1000x1000mm) and door top datum at **2078mm FFL** (verifying 103.5mm head zone under +2273.5mm wall plate).
*   **Threshold Riser & Drainage:** Install a treated **42mm tall x 150mm wide C24 timber riser block** over the DPC across the ~1000mm door opening to bring the door frame base up to **+92mm FFL**. Set an Aco HexDrain channel flush with the external patio level directly in front of the West door, and apply **RIW Flexiseal** liquid moisture barrier over the threshold junction.

### Week 8: Rainscreen Battens, Cladding & Rainwater System
*   **Rainscreen Battens:** Screw **25x50mm treated rainscreen battens @ 400mm centers** vertically over the Tyvek using 150mm A4 EWI screws driven in alternating 60°/90° angles (Truss-Screw method). Staple **Perforated Bug Mesh** across the bottom 25mm gap between battens (cavity air inlet at +50mm). **Also fit the ~18.6 lm top vent trim now:** staple the Perforated Aluminium Vent Trim to the underside of the cantilevered SIP roof soffit, bridging the 25mm batten gap (§2.6 item 5). *Foreman Note: this is the cavity's only air outlet and it MUST go on before the top Hardie plank — once the top course is fixed you cannot reach it.*
*   **dMVHR Batten Frame:** Around the wall pipe, screw short 25mm batten noggins to frame a **~280 × 280mm square** centred on the pipe. Leave gaps at the top and bottom of the frame so the cavity still drains and ventilates.
*   **Cladding Installation:** Install **James Hardie VL Fibre Cement Cladding** across rainscreen battens using Hardie™ Plank Screws (4.2x40mm T15 Torx). Use a standard drill/driver on low torque (no impact drivers).
    *   *dMVHR notch — no round cut needed:* The Zehnder outside panel (380 × 376mm) hides the opening, so cut a **square ~280 × 280mm** hole across the planks that cross the pipe. Mark each plank in place, then take it down and cut on trestles: long straight cuts with the fibre-cement circular saw blade (or score-and-snap), a ~10mm relief hole at each inside corner, and the short cuts joining them with a jigsaw (carbide-grit fibre-cement blade) or multi-tool. 🚨 *Silica: cut outdoors only, on-tool extraction connected, FFP3 mask on.* Leave the outside wall panel off until the unit is hung in Week 12+.
*   **Rainwater Harvesting:** Install 112mm UPVC Deepflow guttering, Sure-Edge drip trims, downpipe diverter, and route to the 200L slimline water butt.

## 🗓️ Phase 4: Internal Thermal Floor & Passivhaus Detailing (Weeks 9-11)
*Now working 100% inside the dry, weather-tight building.*

### Week 9: Solid Timber Diaphragm Deck
*   **The OSB3 Load-Spreader:** Inside the bone-dry room, peel off the Egger Protect protective foil. Apply D4 structural adhesive to the surface and lay 18mm OSB3 T&G directly over the 22mm Egger Protect deck, screwing into joists at 300mm centers to form a solid 40mm laminated timber slab. 
*   **Utility Penetration:** Pull the SWA power and Cat6a data cables through the service cable core hole. Fill the void inside the SIP core with Illbruck FM330 airtight foam and seal the inside face to the SIP OSB with a Pro Clima Kaflex grommet / Tescon Vana tape before the Fermacell goes up.

### Week 10: Internal Passivhaus Airtight Taping
*   **Airtight VCL Taping:** Meticulously tape every internal wall-to-wall, wall-to-roof, and wall-to-floor joint, plus all window and door frame reveals, with **Pro Clima Tescon Vana** tape. Tape the dMVHR wall pipe to the SIP OSB with overlapping Tescon Vana petals all round — this is the airtight seal at the only intentional ventilation penetration. This forms the continuous internal Passivhaus airtight/vapour barrier.

### Week 11: Direct-Mount Internal Boards & Floor Finish
*   **Direct-Mount Fermacell:** Pre-cut 15mm Fermacell boards. Apply Jointstik glue to board edges and screw directly to the taped internal SIP OSB faces using 30mm Fermacell screws. (Provides required REI30/REI60 boundary fire rating).
*   **dMVHR Internal Build-Out:** After the main Fermacell is up (cut neatly round the pipe), build the **60mm local build-out** around the pipe: 45 × 45mm planed treated timber frame screwed through the Fermacell into the SIP OSB, 40mm wood fibre infill, 15mm Fermacell face glued and screwed, boxed tight to the pipe. Size it ≥50mm beyond the unit's mounting plate on every side (~600 × 700mm — use the Zehnder mounting template). Check the finished wall at the pipe measures **≥275mm** (target 296mm).
*   **Industrial Floor Paint:** Apply 2mm high-build industrial polyurethane / epoxy floor paint directly to the 18mm OSB3 (FFL = +92mm).

## 🗓️ Phase 5: MEP, Climate & Final Commissioning (Week 12+)

### Week 12+: Surface Electrics, ASHP, dMVHR & Commissioning
*   **Surface Conduit Electrics:** Run 20mm galvanised steel or PVC conduit and surface-mounted metal-clad sockets directly over the Fermacell. Keep circuits separated on separately labelled RCBOs (20A Type C for machines, 16A Type B for ASHP, 10A Type B for IR/controls, 6A Type B for dMVHR).
*   **dMVHR Installation:** Hang the **Zehnder ComfoAir 70** on the internal build-out over the wall pipe (axis at **+1616.7mm Datum Zero** / **1524.7mm FFL**, 600mm below the +2216.7mm low-side plate). Fit the outside wall panel (380 × 376mm) over the square cladding opening. Fit the **F7** filter option wherever Zehnder permits it (extract side confirmation pending — MASTER_PLAN §4.2). *Foreman Note: this datum leaves 585mm clear above the pipe axis against Zehnder's 550mm minimum — do not raise it. Keep 320mm clear left and 420mm right of the axis.*
*   **TT Island Earthing:** Drive a 1.2m copper earth rod into soil outside near the power entry. Route 16mm² earth cable to the Consumer Unit Earth Bar.
*   **HVAC & Lighting:** Mount ceiling 1.2kW IR panel, surface track lighting, and DIY Quick-Connect ASHP / Mini-Split unit. Connect pre-charged AC lines to external compressor. Place 15mm SBR rubber isolation pads under cast-iron machine feet.
*   **Sign-Off & Commissioning:** Hire a Part P registered electrician (NICEIC/NAPIT) to inspect, terminate main power, and issue Part P certification. Commission dMVHR with a BPEC engineer (respecting the 800mm clear zone in front of the dMVHR).
*   **Statutory Completion:** No Building Control completion (exempt). Collect the electrician's Electrical Installation Certificate and Part P compliance certificate, and file them with the Building Control exemption letter, the Building Notice withdrawal confirmation and the LDC.

## 🛑 The Foreman’s Final Warning
You are building a high-tech thermal envelope, not a shed. 
*   If the OSB gets wet, it swells and structural integrity is compromised. Protect it at all costs.
*   If you skip the Fermacell, the building inspector will condemn the build due to boundary fire regulations.
*   Pace yourself. Eat well. Keep the site clean so you don't trip over a chop saw on a Sunday evening. 

Now get to work.
