# Gap Report & Validation Status

**Date:** 2026-05-23
**Event:** Sloped SIP Roof + Flat 50mm PIR Warm-Over-Roof Pass
**Status:** COMPLIANT AFTER DATUM REDUCTION

## Overview
A validation run was executed against the updated roof schedule. The 1:60 fall is now generated by the SIP roof support geometry/top plates, with a constant-thickness 50mm tissue-faced PIR board bonded above the sloped SIP and the EPDM membrane fully adhered to the PIR.

## Test Suite Results

*   **Test 002 (Roof Structure & Height Limit):** PASS. 
    *   *PD Limit Checked:* The absolute highest point of the building is exactly 2500mm (150mm exposed splash + 2133mm high-side wall + 150mm SIP + 50mm flat PIR + 1.5mm EPDM + 0.5mm VCL + 15mm Fermacell, rounded within construction tolerance). 
    *   *Structural Span:* A 150mm SIP can still structurally span the 3430mm clear width safely within L/240 deflection limits, provided it is joined with insulated splines.
*   **Test 003 (Wall & Vapour):** PASS. The vapor profile is now more robust: the 50mm PIR sits outside the SIP, raising the temperature of the upper SIP OSB skin and reducing moisture accumulation risk at the outer structural layer.
*   **Test 05 (Flush Threshold Moisture):** PASS. The 1:60 roof fall is maintained by the SIP support geometry rather than by tapered PIR. The rainwater path remains directed to the gutter edge.
*   **Head-Clearance Check:** PASS WITH REVISED OPENINGS. The added 50mm flat PIR reduces the high-side wall height to 2133mm and the low-side wall height to 2076mm. The previous 2100mm datum fails because it leaves only 11mm structural head clearance on the high side and negative clearance on the low side. The revised schedule uses a 2000mm West high-side datum and a 1950mm low-side/clerestory datum, preserving approximately 111mm and 104mm structural head zones respectively after the 22mm finished floor is included.

**Conclusion:** The Master Plan and Workplan are updated. Moisture robustness and roof U-value improve. Glazing and door openings remain functional only after reducing the West door/frame height to 2000mm, reducing West panorama windows to 1200x1000mm, and lowering clerestory top datum to 1950mm.
