## Date: 2026-03-19
**Foreman Notes: Open Excavation Weather Protocol**
- User asked about leaving a half-finished dig out open to the elements (likely due to the 12.5-tonne manual labor pacing).
- **The Risk:** A 4.4x5.4m hole at 330mm depth holds roughly 7,800 liters of water. If rain pools inside, it will saturate the 'formation level' (the bare dirt/clay at the bottom of the trench). Saturated subgrade loses its structural CBR (California Bearing Ratio) load capacity. If they lay MOT on wet mud and wacker plate it, it will simply sink ("pumping"), ruining the foundation base.
- **The Mitigation:** Updated the "Sunday Lockdown" step for Weekend 1 in both `WORKPLAN_MODULAR.md` and `WORKPLAN_SIPS.md` with a strict `🚨 OPEN EXCAVATION PROTOCOL`.
- **Instructions Added:**
    1.  **Sump & Pump:** Dig a deeper bucket-sized hole in the lowest corner. If it rains, water flows here for easy extraction via a submersible pump.
    2.  **Tarp the Base:** Pin down heavy-duty tarpaulins specifically over the *exposed flat earth* at the bottom of the trench to prevent rain from turning it to mud.
