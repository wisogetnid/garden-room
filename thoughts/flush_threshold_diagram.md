# Visualizing the Flush Threshold on a Floating Insulated Raft

You are correct to visualize the 100mm Jackodur EPS insulation upstand wrapping the concrete slab. This creates a specific geometric challenge when trying to achieve a flush transition (level patio to interior floor) because that 100mm of soft EPS foam sits exactly where your patio meets your building.

Here is how all the components fit together geometrically from outside to inside at the doorway:

## The "Submarine" Cross-Section (At the Doors)

1.  **The Patio Paving (Outside):** This is your hard landscaping, set at the exact height of your internal finished floor.
2.  **The Aco HexDrain (The Moat):** This plastic channel drain is set into the ground *immediately abutting* the patio paving. Its top grate is perfectly flush with the patio.
3.  **The Jackodur EPS Upstand (100mm thick):** This is the vertical rim of your insulation tub. It sits between the Aco drain and your concrete slab.
    *   *The Problem:* You cannot just leave soft EPS foam exposed to the Aco drain or foot traffic, and you cannot fix a heavy door frame into soft foam.
    *   *The Solution:* The EPS upstand is shaved down so its top edge sits roughly 50mm *below* the finished floor level.
4.  **The Compacfoam CF200 Block (The Bridge):** This high-density structural thermal break (e.g., 140mm wide x 50mm high) is bonded *over* the top of the EPS upstand and overlaps onto the edge of the concrete slab. 
    *   This block spans the soft 100mm EPS gap. 
    *   It provides a solid, rot-proof, highly insulated foundation to screw the heavy door threshold into.
5.  **Liquid Waterproofing (The Seal):** A thick liquid membrane (like RIW Flexiseal) is painted continuously from the side of the Aco drain, up and over the remaining exposed EPS upstand, and completely covering the front face and top of the Compacfoam block. This creates a waterproof "hull."
6.  **The Door Threshold:** The bottom track of your UPVC/Aluminium door sits directly on top of the waterproofed Compacfoam block. It is screwed down through the Compacfoam into the concrete below.
7.  **The Concrete Slab (Inside):** The 100mm reinforced concrete slab sits inside the EPS tub.
8.  **The PVC Floor Tile (7mm):** Laid on the concrete, its top surface aligns perfectly with the top of the door threshold and the outside patio paving.

## Why this works (The Water Path)
If heavy rain hits the glass doors, it runs down the glass, hits the door threshold, drips down the waterproofed face of the Compacfoam/EPS upstand, and falls directly into the Aco drain. 

Because the Aco drain is sitting right against the waterproofed insulation upstand, the water never has a chance to bridge the gap and touch the internal concrete slab or flow under the door. The 100mm EPS upstand remains completely protected from crushing and moisture.
