# Structural Validation: Single vs Double Steel Mesh for Garden Office

**Agent:** @strategist
**Context:** The user is converting the 4x5m garden room use-case from a heavy-duty wood workshop (with 250kg dynamic cast-iron loads) to a standard home office.
**Question:** Does a 150mm concrete slab on a JACKODUR XPS tub require a double layer of A252 steel mesh, or is a single layer sufficient?

## Web Research & Engineering Standards Analysis:
1.  **Standard Domestic Rafts:** For domestic garden rooms and light-duty outbuildings (like a home office), UK building control and structural engineering standards (Eurocode 2) typically specify a single layer of steel reinforcement (often A142, A193, or A252) placed in the bottom third of the slab.
2.  **The Purpose of Double Mesh:** Double layers of mesh are designed for heavy vehicular traffic, industrial point-loading, or suspended structural slabs. In a ground-bearing insulated raft (where the earth and XPS uniformly support the slab), the primary tension is at the bottom of the slab.
3.  **Crack Control & UFH:** The top layer of mesh is usually added to control surface shrinkage cracking, especially when Underfloor Heating (UFH) expands the slab. However, a single layer of heavier A252 mesh placed with 50mm bottom cover, combined with a proper C32/40 mix, is structurally sound to control these shrinkage forces for a small 4x5m footprint.
4.  **The Tolerance Crisis Solved:** By removing the top layer of A252 mesh, we completely eliminate the terrifying 2mm tolerance issue. 
    *   *New Geometry:* 50mm (chairs) + 16mm (A252 mesh) + 16mm (UFH pipe) = 82mm. 
    *   In a 150mm slab, this leaves a massive **68mm of top concrete cover**. This provides complete protection against carbonation (spalling) and guarantees the power float will not strike the steel or the UFH pipes.

## Conclusion:
A single layer of A252 steel mesh is structurally verified as **more than sufficient** for a home office, and actively safer to construct, eliminating the catastrophic top-cover risk. 

**Recommendation:** Update Master Plan and Workplan to specify a Single Layer of A252 mesh.
