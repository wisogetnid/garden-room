# Cost Breakdown: Remaining Build Costs

**As of:** 26 Oct 2026. Supersedes `research/cost_analysis_master_plans.md` (the early three-option comparison).
**Scope:** everything still to buy or pay for to finish the workshop as specified in `plans/MASTER_PLAN.md`.
**Already paid (excluded):** ground screws (No More Digging), planning (LDC), structural engineer (26C070/1).

**Basis:**
- All figures **inc VAT** (20%). A garden room is not a new dwelling, so no zero-rating. Ex-VAT list prices from the supplier files are ×1.2.
- **Fixed figures** come from the user / quotes. **Estimates** come from the supplier lists in `research/suppliers/` (18 Oct 2026 list prices) and a web price check on 26 Oct 2026. Confidence per line is 1–5.
- Isle of Wight delivery surcharges are not in the line prices. They are carried as one allowance in §8.
