# Ring Beam Material List: Jewson vs Sydenhams

**Scope (as of 18 Oct 2026):** the laminated 141 × 125mm timber ring beam (3408 × 4924mm) and the two internal E-W beams, which are built with the same plies and screws (MASTER_PLAN §1.2, §1.2.2). This covers WORKPLAN Weekend 2.

**Not on this list:**
- **Joists, hangers, straps and deck-edge brackets:** see the main foundation list, `comms/Jewson_Materials_List.csv`.
- **Polymeric DPC, sole plate and M12 anchors:** New Forest SIPs supply and fit these.
- **10mm packers:** these come from No More Digging with the RADIX PRO top plates.

**Prices:** web list prices read on 18 Oct 2026, **ex VAT**. Both sites also show the inc-VAT price: Jewson in its page data, Sydenhams beside the ex-VAT price. Trade-account discounts, Isle of Wight delivery and island surcharges are not included. Jewson prices come from the product page data, which is used for a default location, so confirm them at the Newport branch. Confidence 3/5.

**Screw rule:** Torx / SPAX T-STAR (plus) drives only, never Pozi or Phillips. Hex-head coach screws are allowed.

## 3. Findings

1. **[OPEN RISK FLAG: Sydenhams "47 × 125" is 45 × 120mm finished.]** Laminating three plies would give a **135 × 120mm** beam, not the 141 × 125mm section designed on 26C070/1. Consequences:
   - The beam underside would sit at -120mm, so the packers would have to grow to 15mm to keep the top at Datum Zero.
   - The 140mm lamination screws would leave only ~2mm of cover through 135mm of timber after countersinking.
   - The engineer would have to re-approve the section.

   The saving is ~£3 on the like-for-like timber (£5.59 vs £5.65 per metre), so it is not worth it. **Buy the plies from Jewson (PAJT4712, 47 × 125mm actual).**
2. **Neither merchant stocks the structural screws.** The 8 × 140mm lamination screws and 8 × 100mm corner screws still come from SPAX UK or Timber Fixings, as already planned.
3. **The end-grain preservative is cheaper at Jewson** (£15.64 vs £19.49) and can go on the same order as the timber.
4. **Coach screws not needed (30 Oct 2026):** No More Digging supplied angle brackets and 6 × 50mm self-drilling screws for the beam-to-plate fixing. The Sydenhams coach screw lines in §2 are kept for reference only.
5. **Treatment class:** neither site states UC3. Jewson PAJT4712 is UC2, and Sydenhams' page gives no use class. The UC2 vs UC3 question for the engineer (MASTER_PLAN §1.2) is unchanged.
