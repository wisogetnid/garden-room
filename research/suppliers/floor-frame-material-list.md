# Floor Frame & PIR Material List

**Scope (as of 18 Oct 2026):** the floor infill between the ring beam and the internal beams (MASTER_PLAN §1.2 Floor Joists / Primary Insulation, §3.1 Floor Diaphragm Tie). Built in WORKPLAN Weekend 2.
- **Layout:** single joists, 10 lines per bay at ~342mm centres. That gives 295mm clear pockets, so each 1200mm PIR board rips into four strips. There are three bays of ~1453mm clear (N–S) × 3126mm (E–W).
- **Insulation:** 125mm PIR at full joist depth, flush top and bottom, resting on small stainless angle brackets screwed to the joist sides from above.

**Not on this list:**
- **Ring beam, internal beams and their screws:** see `ring-beam-material-list.md`. The 8 × 100mm screws for the edge joists come in the same box of 50 as the corner screws.
- **Sub-deck, birch ply and deck screws:** see `comms/Jewson_Materials_List.csv`.

**Prices:** web list prices read on 18 Oct 2026, **ex VAT**, without trade discount or Isle of Wight delivery. Confidence 3/5. The PIR market is short of stock (several suppliers out of stock or warning of shortages, 18 Oct 2026), so confirm stock and island delivery before ordering.

**Screw rule:** Torx / SPAX T-STAR (plus) drives only.

**PIR cut plan:** rip every board into four ~293mm strips (295mm pockets, ~2mm foam gap). Then:
1. Cut each 2400mm strip into one 1450mm pocket piece and a 950mm offcut. Twenty strips fill 20 pockets.
2. Fill the remaining 7 pockets with a 950mm offcut plus a 500mm piece cut from another 950. Seal each joint with foam.

Measure every board before cutting. A board over 125mm thick stands proud of the joists and rocks the deck: rasp it flush on top and re-tape the foil.

## 3. Changes From the Previous Plan
- **Joists:** 9 lines at ~385mm + 6 twin pieces → **10 single lines at ~342mm**. 30 joists instead of 33, so 10 × 4.8m instead of 11.
- **Hangers:** 58 → **52**, since the twins needed 12.
- **Dropped:** the twin-joist 83mm packers and M10 bolts.
- **PIR:** 6 boards of 120mm on screw pegs (~30% waste) → **5 boards of 125mm** on stainless angle brackets (the fixing band was dropped: it could only be fitted from below). The full depth closes the 5mm air gap under the deck.
- **Pegs:** the ~300 A4 5 × 60mm screw pegs are dropped.
- **Restraint straps and strutting dropped:** the East/West beams are tied in by the edge joists (8 × 140mm screws at 300mm, from the lamination screw order) and the deck (screwed at 150mm into the edge joists). No noggins, so every PIR pocket is clear end to end. **[OPEN RISK FLAG: departs from the 26C070/1 strap note without engineer review; user decision.]**
