## 3. Strategic Financial & Logistical Analysis

### 1. The Isle of Wight "Ferry Tax" (Logistics)
* **Timber Frame:** Requires multiple deliveries from various merchants (timber, insulation, membranes, cladding). Transporting 4.8m lengths of timber and bulky PIR boards to the island incurs heavy freight premiums.
* **EPS SIPs:** Typically manufactured in the Midlands or North. You will pay £500 - £800+ just for the dedicated flatbed delivery and ferry crossing.
* **MgO SIPs (Winner):** Because the `MASTER_PLAN_MODULAR.md` specifies **MTL Ltd in Sandown, Isle of Wight**, you bypass the Solent freight costs entirely. This effectively reduces the MgO SIP total cost by ~£600 compared to mainland imports.

### 2. Time vs. Money (The Self-Builder Equation)
* **Timber Frame (Cheapest Materials, Highest Labor):** At £13,800, this is the cheapest raw material option. However, constructing a timber frame to Passivhaus airtightness requires meticulously cutting and taping hundreds of individual insulation boards and membranes. This will take a weekend DIYer **4 to 6 weeks** of solid work just to reach a watertight shell.
* **EPS SIPs (Most Expensive):** At £17,400, this is the most expensive route because it effectively builds the wall twice (SIPs + Wood Fibre + Batten + Heavy Hardie Cladding). It is overkill for a 20m² footprint.
* **MgO SIPs (The Value Sweet Spot):** At £16,050 (closer to £15,400 when factoring in local island delivery), the MgO SIPs cost £1,600 more than timber but will reach a watertight, highly secure shell in **2 to 3 days**. The elimination of the complex rainscreen and timber cladding saves immense amounts of time.

### 4. Ongoing Maintenance Costs (Lifecycle Cost)
* **Timber Frame:** Larch cladding requires UV oiling every 2-3 years to prevent silvering/patching, or it will degrade in the salty Cowes air. 
* **EPS SIPs (Hardie Plank):** Very low maintenance, but requires occasional washing.
* **MgO SIPs (Silicone Render):** Zero maintenance. The silicone render is hydrophobic (water-repellent) and self-cleaning.

## Conclusion & Recommendation
While the **Traditional Timber Frame** is nominally ~£2,200 cheaper in raw materials, the **MgO SIPs (Modular)** strategy represents the highest return on investment. The higher upfront cost of the MgO panels is heavily offset by the elimination of external rainscreen cladding, zero Solent freight fees, a drastically reduced build time, and a completely maintenance-free exterior suited for the coastal Isle of Wight climate.
