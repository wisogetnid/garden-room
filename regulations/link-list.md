# Isle of Wight Council: Planning & Building Control Links

This document provides the direct links and short descriptions for your garden room project applications.

## 1. Proposed Lawful Development Certificate (LDC)
A legally binding document from the local authority confirming that your outbuilding falls under Permitted Development (Class E) and does not require formal Planning Permission. This is the gold standard of proof for future property sales.
* **Online Application:** [Planning Portal Application Service](https://www.planningportal.co.uk/app)

## 2. Building Regulations Approval
Mandatory for outbuildings with an internal footprint between 15m² and 30m² that sit less than 1m from a boundary. This process ensures structural, electrical, and fire safety compliance, culminating in a Building Regulations Completion Certificate (BRCC).
* **Apply via Planning Portal:** [Building Control Applications Portal](https://www.planningportal.co.uk/applications/building-control-applications/)
* **Direct Council Form (PDF):** [Building Notice Application Form](https://digitalservices.iow.gov.uk/documents/download/building-notice-application-form)

## 3. Pre-Application Planning Advice
A formal service offered by the Isle of Wight Council to discuss your plans with a planning officer before officially submitting them. Useful for getting initial feedback on your high-spec Passivhaus design and boundary proximity.
* **Online Digital Portal:** [Access the Pre-Application Advice Service](https://digitalservices.iow.gov.uk/preapp/application-form/gdpr)
* **Download Form (PDF):** [Pre-Application Advice Form](https://digitalservices.iow.gov.uk/documents/download/pre-application-advice-form)
