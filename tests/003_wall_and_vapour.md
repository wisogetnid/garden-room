# Test 003: Wall Build-Up and Vapour Management

**Requirement:** The structural envelope must utilize continuous insulation (either external over timber frame or integrated via SIPs).
**Rationale:** Chosen for thermal performance, breathability, and flexibility. 
**Constraint:** Must include a meticulously taped internal Vapour Control Layer (VCL) as the primary defense against interstitial condensation.
**Validation:** 
1. The master plan must detail the wall build-up, specify continuous insulation, and explicitly include the taped internal VCL.
2. **Active Compliance Validation:** The Test Runner MUST actively use web searches to verify the vapour permeability and interstitial condensation risk of the exact proposed wall 'sandwich' (e.g., MgO/OSB SIPs + Wood Fibre + VCL + specific breather membranes) against UK coastal weather data and Passivhaus airtightness standards. Ensure the combination prevents moisture trapping.
