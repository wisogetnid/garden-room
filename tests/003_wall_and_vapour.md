# Test 003: Wall Build-Up and Vapour Management

**Requirement:** The structural envelope must be a Traditional Timber Frame (single frame) with external continuous insulation.
**Rationale:** Chosen for breathability, flexibility, and DIY modification. Twin-stud is rejected.
**Constraint:** Must include a meticulously taped internal Vapour Control Layer (VCL) as the primary defense against interstitial condensation.
**Validation:** The master plan must detail the single timber frame build-up, specify external continuous insulation, and explicitly include the taped internal VCL.
