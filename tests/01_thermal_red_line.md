# The 'Red Line' Thermal Continuity Test
**Type:** Structural Physics / Passivhaus Compliance
**Target Agent:** `@architect`

## Objective
Verify that the highly insulated Passivhaus thermal envelope (the "Red Line") is completely continuous from the foundation raft, up the walls, and across the roof without a single thermal bridge.

## Verification Checklist

### 1. The Foundation-to-Wall Junction (The Weakest Link)
- [ ] Does the external wall insulation (SIP core or external wood fibre) perfectly align with, or overlap, the vertical foundation perimeter insulation (e.g., JACKODUR XPS)?
- [ ] Is the traditional timber sole plate replaced by a thermally broken, high-density load-bearing material (e.g., Compacfoam CF200)?
- [ ] Is there explicit confirmation that the concrete slab does NOT touch the external skin of the wall structure (preventing cold flanking)?

### 2. Panel Joints & Splines
- [ ] Are all wall and roof SIP panels joined using **Insulated Splines** (rather than solid timber C16/C24 structural timber)?
- [ ] Is there an explicit instruction for the builder to use expanding structural polyurethane adhesive/foam deep inside the spline rebate before joining?

### 3. Wall-to-Roof Junction
- [ ] Does the roof insulation layer perfectly intersect or lap over the wall insulation layer?
- [ ] If the roof has a 50cm overhang, is the cantilever detailed to ensure the cold overhang does not conduct back into the warm internal zone?

## Failure States (Do Not Pass If:)
- A standard timber sole plate sits directly on cold concrete bridging to the outside.
- Solid timber C24 splines are specified every 1200mm in the SIP walls (creating a massive repeating thermal bridge).
- The U-Value calculated for the wall assembly exceeds 0.15 W/m²K.
