# Test 004: Door Access Strategy

**Requirement:** The primary access must be an Asymmetric "1.5" Door system (1000mm main active leaf + 400mm-600mm bolted slave leaf) OR a premium Lift-and-Slide system.
**Rationale:** Ensures Passivhaus-level sealing for acoustics and airtightness while allowing a 1.4m+ clear opening for machinery and 8x4 sheet goods.
**Constraint:** French Doors are strictly eliminated due to acoustic and airtightness failures at the meeting stile.
**Validation:** 
1. The master plan must explicitly forbid French doors and specify the approved door access type.
2. **Active Acoustic/Air Validation:** The Test Runner MUST actively use web searches to cross-reference the specific proposed door type (e.g., asymmetric 1.5 doors or lift-and-slide) against Passivhaus airtightness standards (Q50/n50 limits) and acoustic decibel (dB) reduction ratings. Validate that the specified door system genuinely outperforms French doors at the meeting stile.
