# Test 001: Foundation Strategy

**Requirement:** The foundation must be an Insulated Concrete Raft (baseline: 150mm slab, 50mm sand blinding, and two layers of A252 mesh sandwiching the UFH pipes).
**Rationale:** Provides extreme thermal mass, maximum vibration dampening for heavy machinery, and structural strength for high point-loads, while allowing for a flush transition to the existing outside patio.
**Constraint:** Must budget for a line pump and small contractor crew for the pour (~3.5 cubic meters) to avoid physical exhaustion and cold-joints.
**Validation:** 
1. The master plan must explicitly specify the layers of an insulated concrete raft foundation (sub-base, blinding, insulation tub, steel mesh, concrete) and include the line pump in the logistics.
2. **Structural Soundness Check:** The Test Runner MUST actively validate that the proposed foundation layers are structurally sound for a high-load wood workshop. Use web searches to cross-reference the proposed concrete thickness, insulation compressive strength (e.g., XPS 300 kPa), and steel reinforcement (e.g., double A252 mesh) against standard UK building engineering practices to ensure they can safely support the 2.5-ton static envelope weight plus the heavy dynamic point-loads of cast-iron machinery without cracking or subsiding.
