# Test 001: Foundation Strategy

**Requirement:** The foundation must be an Insulated Concrete Raft.
**Rationale:** Provides maximum thermal mass, vibration dampening for heavy machinery, and allows for a flush transition to the existing outside patio.
**Constraint:** Must budget for a line pump and small contractor crew for the 2 cubic meter pour to avoid physical exhaustion and cold-joints.
**Validation:** The master plan must explicitly specify an insulated concrete raft foundation and include the line pump in the logistics/budget section.
