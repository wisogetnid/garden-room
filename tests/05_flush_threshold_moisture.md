# The Flush Threshold Moisture Defense Test
**Type:** Hydrological Physics / Capillary Control
**Target Agent:** `@architect`

## Objective
Verify that the critical, high-risk "flush threshold" geometry (patio-to-interior) uses 100% capillary-resistant materials to physically block wind-driven coastal rain from wicking into the structural wall base, specifically for a 20m² flat-roofed Cowes workspace.

## Verification Checklist

### 1. Zero-Threshold Drainage Geometry
- [ ] Is an Aco HexDrain (or linear channel drain) specified to sit directly flush against the rendered foundation upstand at the doorway?
- [ ] Are 200mm wide French Drains filled with 20mm washed gravel specified around all non-door elevations to handle roof runoff and coastal downpours instantly?

### 2. Capillary Wicking (The Submarine Layer)
- [ ] Because the internal floor is flush with the wet patio, is the traditional timber sole plate replaced with a "submarine layer" (e.g., **Compacfoam CF200**) sitting on the concrete edge to prevent rot?
- [ ] Is liquid-applied Waterproof Membrane (e.g., RIW Flexiseal) painted heavily over the XPS upstand and lapping up the Compacfoam block?
- [ ] Is the Damp Proof Membrane (1200-gauge DPM) folded up the concrete edge, sitting *beneath* the Compacfoam, and lapped beneath the upper DPC by min 100mm, sealed with Butyl Tape?

### 3. Roof Rainwater Harvesting & Downpours
- [ ] Does the 20m² flat roof incorporate a dedicated fall (e.g., 1:60 tapered PIR) directing water to a specific edge to prevent pooling?
- [ ] Is the 112mm UPVC Deepflow guttering explicitly routed to a Rainwater Diverter and a 200L-250L Slimline Water Butt to mitigate shock loads on ground drainage during heavy storms?

## Failure States (Do Not Pass If:)
- The flush doorway threshold sits directly on a C16 timber baseplate that will rot when patio water touches it.
- A 200L water butt is installed without a safe, designated overflow strategy (e.g., routing excess away from the concrete foundation).
- The EPDM flat roof does not explicitly specify a fall direction (tapered boards).
