# Test 007: Construction Timeline

**Requirement:** The construction schedule must include a strict 4-week halt immediately following the foundation pour.
**Rationale:** Allows full curing and drying of the concrete raft before timber framing begins, mitigating moisture risk to the timber.
**Constraint:** No timber framing or wall construction can occur during this period.
**Validation:** The master plan's timeline or scheduling section must explicitly list a 4-week curing halt after the concrete pour phase.
