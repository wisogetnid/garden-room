# Test 007: Construction Timeline

**Requirement:** The construction schedule must include a strict concrete curing or halting period prior to heavy loading.
**Rationale:** Allows sufficient curing of the concrete raft before heavy point-loads (like SIP walls or timber framing) are applied.
**Constraint:** No heavy structural erection can occur immediately after the pour.
**Validation:** 
1. The master plan's timeline or scheduling section must explicitly list a curing halt after the concrete pour phase.
2. **Active Engineering Validation:** The Test Runner MUST actively use web searches to verify the required curing time for the specific volume and depth of the proposed concrete mix (e.g., 150mm C25/30 mix) under typical UK seasonal temperatures. It must cross-reference this curing curve to guarantee the concrete has reached sufficient MPa compressive strength before the Workplan permits loading the heavy SIP walls or timber frame onto the slab perimeter.
