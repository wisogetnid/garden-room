# Test 006: Dust Extraction System

**Requirement:** The dust extraction system must utilize an internal recirculation system.
**Rationale:** To preserve the thermal and acoustic envelope of the building, no external venting of dust collection air is permitted.
**Constraint:** Must incorporate high-grade HEPA filtration and cyclone separation.
**Validation:** 
1. The master plan must detail an internal, non-venting dust extraction system specifying HEPA and cyclone components.
2. **Active Safety Validation:** The Test Runner MUST actively use web searches to validate the proposed dust extraction system against ATEX explosive dust regulations and respiratory health standards. It must verify that the specified rigid ducting (e.g., galvanized steel vs PVC) and grounding methods safely eliminate static build-up, and that the specified HEPA filters effectively capture fine hardwood dust (<PM2.5) during internal recirculation.
