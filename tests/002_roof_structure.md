# Test 002: Roof Structure

**Requirement:** The roof must utilize a "Warm Roof" methodology or SIP roof structure.
**Rationale:** Eliminates roof condensation risks while prioritizing thermal performance.
**Constraint:** All insulation must be entirely *on top* of the timber joists or integrated into the SIP. The overall building height must remain strictly under the 2.5m Permitted Development limit.
**Validation:** 
1. The master plan must clearly define the roof build-up with insulation above the joists (or continuous SIP) and explicitly state the total external height is ≤ 2.5m. If using heavy SIP panels, the plan must specify splitting 1200mm panels into 600mm narrow-format ones or hiring a material lift.
2. **Active Structural Validation:** The Test Runner MUST actively use web searches to cross-reference the proposed roof span (e.g., 4m) against the structural load-bearing limits and deflection thresholds of the specified roof materials (e.g., 172mm/175mm SIP panels or chosen timber joist dimensions). It must also verify that the entire build-up thickness combined with the internal ceiling height requirements strictly complies with the 2.5m UK Permitted Development limit.
