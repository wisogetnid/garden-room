# The 'Weather-Tight' Sequence Test
**Type:** Logistical Physics / Moisture Defense
**Target Agent:** `@foreman`

## Objective
Verify that the `WORKPLAN` sequencing strictly protects water-sensitive materials (like OSB and open structural joints) from coastal wind-driven rain, accounting for the unpredictable weather of the Isle of Wight.

## Verification Checklist

### 1. The Sunday Lockdown
- [ ] Are all critical exposed structural elements legally "weather-tight" by Sunday at 4:00 PM before the DIY builder returns to work on Monday?
- [ ] Is there an explicit instruction to secure heavy-duty tarps if the structure is incomplete over the weekend?

### 2. Moisture-Sensitive Core Protection
- [ ] If specifying an OSB-faced SIP structure: Does the schedule mandate the immediate application of a breather membrane (e.g., Tyvek UV Facade) on the same day the panels are erected? **(Zero-Delay Protocol)**
- [ ] If the panels are MgO (Magnesium Oxide) SIPs: Are they explicitly confirmed to be inorganic and impervious to coastal moisture swelling?

### 3. Roof Sealing Timelines
- [ ] Is the primary EPDM/GPR waterproofing membrane applied immediately after the roof panels are lifted and taped?
- [ ] Are the Drip Edge Trims (Sure-Edge UPVC) installed as part of the 'Weather-Tight' lockdown?

## Failure States (Do Not Pass If:)
- The Workplan instructs the builder to leave naked OSB SIPs exposed for an entire week while waiting for breather membranes or cladding.
- The roof panels are lifted on Weekend 5, but the EPDM rubber deck isn't installed until Weekend 6 without an emergency tarp protocol.
- There are unsealed or untaped external spline joints left exposed to rain.
