# Test 005: Heating and Flooring Strategy

**Requirement:** The heating strategy must combine an Internal Electric Flow Boiler for underfloor heating (UFH) and an overhead Infrared (IR) panel.
**Rationale:** The Electric Flow Boiler powers the steady-state thermal battery (e.g., 14°C) in the concrete slab without requiring any external Passivhaus envelope penetrations (unlike an ASHP), while the IR panel provides instant localized heat for the occupant.
**Constraint:** Flooring must be heavy-duty interlocking PVC tiles (e.g., 7mm Ecotile) or hard-bonded engineered wood for tool safety and machinery point-loads.
**Validation:** 
1. The master plan must include the dual heating system (Electric Boiler + IR panel) and specify the heavy-duty flooring over the slab.
2. **Active Performance Validation:** The Test Runner MUST actively use web searches to verify the W/m² thermal output of the proposed UFH + Boiler combination against the estimated Passivhaus heat load (e.g., 1.5kW-2.0kW for 20m²). Furthermore, it must cross-reference the compressive strength and point-load capacity of the specified flooring (e.g., 7mm PVC Ecotile or bonded engineered oak) against the extreme static weights of cast-iron woodworking machinery (e.g., 250kg table saws).
