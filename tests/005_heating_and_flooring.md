# Test 005: Heating and Flooring Strategy

**Requirement:** The heating strategy must combine a high-efficiency ASHP for underfloor heating (UFH) and an overhead Infrared (IR) panel.
**Rationale:** ASHP/UFH provides a steady-state thermal battery (e.g., 14°C) in the concrete slab, while the IR panel provides instant localized heat for the occupant.
**Constraint:** Flooring must be heavy-duty interlocking PVC tiles (e.g., 7mm Ecotile) for tool safety and machinery point-loads, loose-laid for future conversion.
**Validation:** The master plan must include the dual heating system (ASHP + IR panel) and specify the 7mm heavy-duty PVC interlocking tiles over the slab.
